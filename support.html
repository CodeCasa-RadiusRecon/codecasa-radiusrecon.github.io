Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Radius Recon has no sign-up and no login. Everything works offline, and your data stays on your device.

Where are my projects stored? Are they backed up?

On your iPhone, inside the app. They are included in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup). To move a project to another device, use Exports → Project Package and send the file via AirDrop, Mail, or Files.

What does Radius Recon Pro unlock?

The free version includes one project, 25 captures in it, and two floor plan scans. Pro is a one-time purchase — not a subscription — that removes those limits. Restore a previous purchase from Settings → Radius Recon Pro → Restore Purchases.

Why don't I see the Floor Plan button?

Floor plan scanning needs a LiDAR sensor, which is present on iPhone Pro models (12 Pro and later). On other iPhones the button is hidden, since the feature cannot work there.

Are floor plans accurate measurements?

No, and the app never presents them as such. A floor plan is an approximate sketch showing the shape and connection of rooms so a reader can see where a finding was. No dimensions, areas, or scale are displayed anywhere, and the app does not measure anything. The photo annotation "measure" tool likewise only displays a value you type in yourself.

My captures have no location

Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services and confirm Radius Recon is allowed. Indoors, GPS accuracy is often poor; the app warns you when a fix is weak and lets you place or correct a pin by hand on the Project Map.

Can I control the location detail in an exported report?

Yes. Every export offers Exact, Approximate (about 110 m), or No Coordinates, so you can share a report without revealing a precise address.

Do I have to use the AI features?

No. They are entirely optional. The app ships with an offline demo provider that produces clearly-labeled placeholder text; connecting a real AI service is your choice, and the key you provide is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a capture, a project, or the whole app — each removes its data and media from your device. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
