Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. RadiusON has no sign-up and no login. Everything works offline, and your data stays on your device.

Where are my projects stored? Are they backed up?

On your iPhone, inside the app. They are included in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup). To move a project to another device, use Exports → Project Package and send the file via AirDrop, Mail, or Files.

What does RadiusON Pro unlock?

The free version includes one project with up to 25 captures. Everything else — the full camera, the map, every export format and the AI tools — is already included. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, that removes those two limits. Restore a previous purchase from Settings → RadiusON Pro → Restore Purchases.

How do I get back to a finding?

Open the pin or capture and tap Return to Finding. It shows the straight-line distance and direction from where you stand, with an arrow when the compass is steady. For a walking or driving route, tap Apple Maps on the same screen.

Are the map measurements accurate?

They are approximate map measurements, and the app labels them that way. They are not surveys, slope distances, or indoor measurements, and an area you draw is a sketch, not a legal boundary. The photo annotation "measure" tool only displays a value you type in yourself.

My captures have no location

Location is asked for when you drop your first GPS pin, and is used only while the app is open. Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services and confirm RadiusON is allowed. Indoors, GPS accuracy is often poor; the app warns you when a fix is weak and lets you place or correct a pin by hand on the Project Map.

Can I control the location detail in an exported report?

Yes. Every export offers Exact, Approximate (about 110 m), or No Coordinates, so you can share a report without revealing a precise location. GeoJSON and KML files, which are nothing but locations, are not made at No Coordinates.

Do I have to use the AI features?

No. They are entirely optional. The app ships with an offline demo provider that produces clearly-labeled placeholder text; connecting a real AI service is your choice, and the key you provide is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a capture, a project, or the whole app — each removes its data and media from your device. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
