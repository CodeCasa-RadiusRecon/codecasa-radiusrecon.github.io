Radius Recon is an iPhone app for people who document places for a living — inspectors, real-estate professionals, contractors, insurance adjusters, journalists, and location scouts.

Walk a site once and leave with the report. Drop a pin where you find something, tag it, attach the photos and notes that document it, and export a finished PDF or interactive web report before you're back in the car.

The Pin Is the Finding. Every pin tells you what was found there — its category, its priority, and the evidence attached to it.

What it does

  • Photos, video, audio notes, typed notes, and GPS markers — each stamped with time and location as you capture it.
  • Findings organized by area, drawn on a map with your walking route.
  • LiDAR floor plans on supported iPhones, with your findings placed on the drawing.
  • Reports as PDF, interactive HTML, CSV, or a complete data bundle.
  • Works fully offline. No account, no sign-up, no tracking.

Privacy in one line

Everything stays on your device unless you export it. There is no developer server and no analytics — see the privacy policy for the details.
