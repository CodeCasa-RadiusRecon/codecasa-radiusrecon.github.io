Effective date: October 9, 2026

RadiusON is a local-first field documentation app. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your data stays on your device. We don't collect it, we can't see it, and we don't want it.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you create in RadiusON — projects, photos, videos, audio recordings, notes, tags, transcripts, GPS coordinates, and areas you draw on the map — is stored only on your device. RadiusON has no user accounts, no cloud service, and no developer server. We (the developer) have no access to anything you capture.

Data we collect

None. RadiusON contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that transmit data. We do not collect personal information, usage data, identifiers, or diagnostics.

Device permissions and why the app asks

  • Location ("While Using the App" only) — asked for when you drop your first GPS pin. It stamps captures and pins with GPS coordinates, shows where you are on the project map, and guides you back to a finding. The app uses location only while a screen that needs it is open, never in the background, and never records a route. Location data is stored on your device only.
  • Camera — to take photos and video for your projects.
  • Microphone — to record audio notes.
  • Photos (add-only) — only if you enable "Save Captures to Photos"; the app can add your captures to your photo library but cannot read it.
  • Speech recognition — only if you use audio transcription. Audio may be processed by Apple's Speech service on-device or on Apple's servers, per Apple's own privacy terms. We never receive it.

Every permission is optional; the app remains usable if you decline.

Data that leaves your device — only when you choose

  • Exports and sharing. When you export a report (PDF, Word, web page, ZIP, project package, GeoJSON or KML) and share it, the data goes wherever you send it. That transfer is entirely under your control, and the Location Privacy setting decides how precise any coordinates in it are.
  • Apple Maps services, when you ask. The map itself comes from Apple. When you look up an address, search for a place, list nearby places, open Look Around, or ask for directions, the coordinate or search text needed for that request is sent to Apple and handled under Apple's privacy terms. Nothing is sent until you tap, and nothing is sent to us.
  • Optional AI provider. RadiusON can send capture content (text, audio, or images you select) to an AI endpoint that you configure yourself in Settings, for features like summarization or transcription. No endpoint is configured by default. Until you add one, the AI tools use Apple Intelligence on your iPhone where it is available, which keeps everything on the device, and are otherwise switched off. We have no relationship with — and no access to — whatever service you choose. Review that provider's privacy policy before use. Your API key for such a service is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.

Data retention and deletion

All data lives on your device, so retention is under your control. Deleting a capture deletes its media files; deleting a project deletes everything in it, including all media; deleting the app deletes all app data. There is nothing to delete on our side, because we never had it.

Children

RadiusON is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled (for example, an optional sync service), this policy will be updated and the effective date changed before that version ships.
