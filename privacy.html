Effective date: July 27, 2026

Radius Recon is a local-first field documentation app. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your data stays on your device. We don't collect it, we can't see it, and we don't want it.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you create in Radius Recon — projects, photos, videos, audio recordings, notes, tags, transcripts, GPS coordinates, and journey routes — is stored only on your device. Radius Recon has no user accounts, no cloud service, and no developer server. We (the developer) have no access to anything you capture.

Data we collect

None. Radius Recon contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that transmit data. We do not collect personal information, usage data, identifiers, or diagnostics.

Device permissions and why the app asks

  • Location — to stamp captures with GPS coordinates and to draw your route on a map while you record a journey. Optional "Always" access is requested only if you enable background journey tracking in Settings (off by default). Location data is stored on your device only.
  • Camera — to take photos and video for your projects, and to scan floor plans on supported devices.
  • Microphone — to record audio notes.
  • Photos (add-only) — only if you enable "Save Captures to Photos"; the app can add your captures to your photo library but cannot read it.
  • Speech recognition — only if you use audio transcription. Audio may be processed by Apple's Speech service on-device or on Apple's servers, per Apple's own privacy terms. We never receive it.

Every permission is optional; the app remains usable if you decline.

Data that leaves your device — only when you choose

  • Exports and sharing. When you export a report (PDF, JSON, ZIP, CSV) and share it, the data goes wherever you send it. That transfer is entirely under your control.
  • Optional AI provider. Radius Recon can send capture content (text, audio, or images you select) to an AI endpoint that you configure yourself in Settings, for features like summarization or transcription. No endpoint is configured by default, the app ships with an offline demo provider, and we have no relationship with — and no access to — whatever service you choose. Review that provider's privacy policy before use. Your API key for such a service is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.

Data retention and deletion

All data lives on your device, so retention is under your control. Deleting a capture deletes its media files; deleting a project deletes everything in it, including all media; deleting the app deletes all app data. There is nothing to delete on our side, because we never had it.

Children

Radius Recon is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled (for example, an optional sync service), this policy will be updated and the effective date changed before that version ships.
